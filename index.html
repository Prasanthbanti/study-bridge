FAQ

Questions Students Usually Ask

How do I choose the right country? +

Country selection depends on your course, academic profile, budget, preferred lifestyle, career goals and other requirements. Speak with a counsellor for guidance specific to your profile.

Can StudyBridge help me choose a university? +

Yes. The university selection process can consider your preferred country, program, academic profile, budget and intake.

Do you provide education loan guidance? +

StudyBridge provides guidance related to education loan planning and the information students may need during the process.

When should I start planning? +

It is useful to begin planning well before your intended intake so you have enough time for course selection, applications, finances, visa preparation and travel planning.

Can I speak with a counsellor? +

Yes. Submit the enquiry form below and the StudyBridge team can contact you.
